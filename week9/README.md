# Body as a Controller 

---

For my project this week I decided to use hand tracking to try to make a way to make simple music in the web. I thought about the ways that you might typically play an instrument. I started by making a p5 version of the theremin that we talked about in class. Then, I moved on to an interface that plays a specific frequency when the user pinches their fingers in a designated space on the screen. Then, when they move it up and not it changes the brightness of the tone. I like the idea of moving your body in space to create music or as an input. The emphasis on movement reminds me of my time as a dancer growing up. For most of my childhood and teenage years, movement was a main language and way of expression for me, so it makes sense to me to integrate this into my work now. I think it helps a user feel more connected to what they are doing, more actively part of the action than if they were clicking a mouse or tapping a screen. I think it also helps the user to focus on one thing. It might be harder to get distracted when more of your body is involved in controlling the interface than if you are just tapping your finger on a screen. I wonder how workflows or entertainment might change in the future to incorporate more gestural control rather than tactile control.
